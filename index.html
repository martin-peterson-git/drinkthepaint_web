Hi, I'm Martin.

I got involved with this hobby in early 1999. The game was cool, the models were cooler and I seemed to have a knack for painting. I built a small Blood Angels army and that was it. My faint interest soon bloomed to an absolute obsession. I borrowed some old issues of White Dwarf from a friend, and found out about the ‘Eavy Metal painters, and Golden Demon. In October 2002, my third consecutive year entering the competition, I walked away with a Bronze in the Duel category.

Flash forward to 2010. After a few slow hobby years, I’d gotten to know Max Faleij as the guy in the Stockholm Games workshop store who was really into painting, and we met Roger Axelsson and Sten Frödin at an annual painting competition hosted by the store. The four of us started painting together in the store after hours, and eventually going to Golden Demon every year. After Max won Gold in the Open competition in 2014, he made the move to Nottingham to join the ‘Eavy Metal team. I eventually joined him, and became an ‘Eavy metal painter myself in June of 2017. I worked there for about five years.

My time in the design studio saw many hobby highlights: from seeing my first official box art on a shelf, to painting milestone kits like Angron, to developing the new Sisters of Battle and Votann ranges. I’m still extremely proud of my work there, and I consider myself an ‘Eavy Metal painter even though I don’t actually work for GW anymore.

Nowadays I still paint as much as I can. I still compete in Golden Demon. I do odd commissions, and teach painting through classes and one-on-one coaching. I really love helping others on their way in the hobby. Aside from sharing what I know, I also learn a lot myself from it.
